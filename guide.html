Visitor guide

Good to know before you go

The practical details that make a first visit smooth — weather, getting here, money, safety and who to call.

When to visit & weather

  • Best months: March–May and October–November — warm, drier and less crowded.
  • Summer (Jun–Sep): Hot and humid, highs near 90°F, with brief afternoon storms.
  • Winter (Dec–Feb): Mild, highs in the 70s°F — prime manatee and festival season.
  • Hurricane season: June 1–November 30; check forecasts and follow local guidance if a storm is named.

Getting here

  • Fly: Tampa International Airport (TPA), about 15 minutes from downtown; St. Pete–Clearwater (PIE) serves the beaches.
  • Drive: I-75 runs north–south, I-4 connects to Orlando, I-275 crosses the bay.
  • Train: Amtrak's Silver Star stops at Tampa Union Station downtown.
  • Parking: Downtown garages and the Fort Brooke garage are handy for Riverwalk days; beach parking fills early on weekends.

Money, language & access

  • Sales tax: 7.5% in Hillsborough County; hotel stays add a tourist tax.
  • Tipping: 18–20% at sit-down restaurants; a dollar or two per drink at bars.
  • Language: Spanish is widely spoken, especially in Ybor City.
  • Accessibility: Major attractions are wheelchair-friendly; free beach wheelchairs are available at Clearwater Beach and other guarded beaches — ask a lifeguard.
  • International visitors: Free Wi-Fi is common in hotels, the airport and cafés; a US eSIM or prepaid SIM works at the airport and phone stores.

Staying safe & comfortable

  • Drink water often and take shade breaks — Florida heat is stronger than it feels.
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen and reapply after swimming; swim near lifeguards and heed beach flag warnings.
  • Give alligators space in parks and lakes — never feed them, and keep pets back from the water's edge.
  • Afternoon lightning is serious: when thunder roars, head indoors.

Hospitals & urgent care

  • Tampa General Hospital — Davis Islands, just south of downtown; the region's Level I trauma center.
  • St. Joseph's Hospital — West Tampa on Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard, with a dedicated children's hospital.
  • AdventHealth Tampa — North Tampa on Fletcher Avenue, near USF and Busch Gardens.
  • Urgent care: For minor illness or injury, urgent care centers (AdventHealth Centra Care, BayCare Urgent Care and others) are spread across the bay area and beaches, with evening hours.

Visitor centers & maps

  • Visit Tampa Bay's visitor center in downtown Tampa stocks maps, brochures and local advice.
  • The Ybor City visitor center on 8th Avenue covers the historic district.

Tampa stories

A quick history of the bay

History

The cigar city

In 1885, Vicente Martinez-Ybor moved his cigar factory to Tampa. Cuban, Spanish and Italian workers rolled millions of cigars by hand, and the brick streets of Ybor City still carry their languages, clubs and recipes.

Legend

The Gasparilla legend

Since 1904, Tampa has celebrated the mythical pirate José Gaspar with a mock invasion: a fully rigged pirate ship sails into the bay, the mayor surrenders the key to the city, and a parade of pirates follows.

Landmark

The Plant Hall story

Railroad magnate Henry B. Plant opened the Tampa Bay Hotel in 1891 — a Moorish-style palace with silver minarets. Today it is Plant Hall at the University of Tampa, with a museum inside.
