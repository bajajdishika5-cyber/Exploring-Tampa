Plan by month

A year in Tampa Bay

Signature seasons at a glance. The Ybor City Saturday Market runs weekly year-round; holiday lights take over the Riverwalk and ZooTampa each December.

January

Gasparilla season begins — Children's Parade, then the Pirate Fest invasion and Parade of Pirates late in the month.

February

Florida State Fair at the fairgrounds, plus the start of Yankees spring training in Tampa.

March

Gasparilla Festival of the Arts and Gasparilla Music Festival; spring training in full swing.

April

Mild beach weather, outdoor concerts and food festivals across the bay area.

May

Tampa Riverfest opens the month; sunset festivals begin at Pier 60.

June

Summer nights at Busch Gardens, Pride celebrations, and warm Gulf water.

July

Fourth of July fireworks over the bay and Boom by the Bay celebrations downtown.

August

Back-to-school season, indoor museum days, and late-summer food truck rallies.

September

Buccaneers season kicks off; thinner crowds and warm seas at the beaches.

October

Guavaween and Halloween events in Ybor City; stone crab season opens mid-month.

November

Manatees return to Apollo Beach as the water cools; holiday markets begin.

December

Winter Village at Curtis Hixon Park, ZooTampa's Christmas in the Wild, and the holiday boat parades.

Entertainment & nightlife

Tampa after dark

The Riverwalk glows, the stages light up, and Ybor's 7th Avenue stays lively late.

Live music

Stages big and small

Arena concerts downtown, amphitheatre shows at the fairgrounds, and live sets nightly in Ybor City clubs and Armature Works.

Comedy & film

Comedy clubs & movie nights

Stand-up at the Tampa Improv in Ybor, classic and indie film at the historic Tampa Theatre, and giant-screen documentaries at MOSI's IMAX.

Evenings out

Rooftops & sunsets

Rooftop bars in downtown and Hyde Park, an evening Riverwalk stroll past the lit bridges, and sunset viewpoints at Ballast Point Park and Pier 60.
