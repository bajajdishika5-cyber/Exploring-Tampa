More ways to visit

Trips for every traveler

Pick a style and follow the plan — each one is built from the places on this page.

Three days in Tampa Bay

  1. Day 1 — Downtown & Ybor: Florida Aquarium in the morning, Riverwalk stroll, streetcar to Ybor City and dinner at the Columbia.
  2. Day 2 — Thrills & animals: A full day at Busch Gardens, or ZooTampa in the morning and Adventure Island after lunch.
  3. Day 3 — The Gulf: Drive to Clearwater Beach, take a dolphin cruise, and finish with the sunset celebration at Pier 60.

Family weekend

  1. Saturday: Glazer Children's Museum and the splash pads at Curtis Hixon Park, then the aquarium next door.
  2. Sunday: ZooTampa in the cool morning, an afternoon Pirate Water Taxi ride, and ice cream at Sparkman Wharf.
  3. Good to know: The streetcar is fare-free and stroller-friendly, and most big attractions offer multi-attraction passes.

Couples trip

  1. Day 1: Coffee in Hyde Park Village, the Tampa Museum of Art, and a sunset cruise from the downtown marina.
  2. Evening: Dinner in Ybor City, then a classic film at the Tampa Theatre or a show at the Straz Center.
  3. Day 2: A slow beach morning at St. Pete Beach, the Dalí Museum in the afternoon, and a rooftop drink back in Tampa.

Foodie tour

  1. Morning: Café con leche and guava pastries in Ybor, then the Saturday Market.
  2. Midday: A pressed Cuban sandwich and Spanish bean soup at the Columbia; food-hall grazing at Armature Works.
  3. Evening: A grouper sandwich by the water, craft beer at Cigar City Brewing, and stone crab in season (mid-October to spring).

Rainy-day plan

  1. Morning: MOSI's exhibits and planetarium, or the Florida Aquarium — both are fully indoors.
  2. Afternoon: Tampa Bay History Center and the Henry B. Plant Museum, with the streetcar running between downtown stops.
  3. Evening: A matinee at the Tampa Theatre, then dinner under cover at Sparkman Wharf or Armature Works.

Budget trip

  1. Free anchors: The Riverwalk, Bayshore Boulevard, the fare-free streetcar and the TECO Manatee Viewing Center in season.
  2. Low-cost days: State-park beaches like Honeymoon Island charge a small per-car entry; pack a picnic from a local bakery.
  3. Smart spending: Look for museum free days, city attraction passes, and happy-hour menus in Ybor and Hyde Park.

Pre-cruise day

  1. Arrive the day before: Stay downtown, in the Channel District or in Ybor City — all just minutes from Port Tampa Bay's cruise terminals.
  2. Spend the day: The Florida Aquarium beside the port in the morning, lunch at Sparkman Wharf, then the fare-free streetcar to Ybor City for the afternoon.
  3. Sailing day: Check your boarding documents for your terminal (2, 3 or 6), arrive at your assigned check-in time, and park in the port's garage or lot. Full details are in the Cruises section.

Bonus exploring

Day trips from Tampa Bay

Tampa Bay is home base — but some of Florida's best days out sit within 150 miles. Distances are approximate, one way from downtown Tampa.

DestinationDistanceWhy go
Tarpon Springs sponge docks~30 miGreek sponge-diving heritage, waterfront shops and Greek bakeries and restaurants.
Weeki Wachee Springs~50 miThe famous live mermaid shows and a crystal-clear spring for kayaking.
Sarasota & Siesta Key~60 miSiesta Key's quartz sand, plus Sarasota's arts scene and the Ringling museum.
Anna Maria Island~55 miOld-Florida beach towns, a fishing pier and sunset over the Gulf.
LEGOLAND Florida~60 miBrick-built theme park in Winter Haven, made for younger kids.
Bok Tower Gardens~70 miA singing carillon tower in landscaped gardens near Lake Wales.
Orlando theme parks~85 miWalt Disney World, Universal and SeaWorld — an easy I-4 drive.
Crystal River~85 miSwim-with-manatee tours and the Three Sisters Springs boardwalk.
Fort Myers~130 miThe Edison & Ford Winter Estates and a gateway to Sanibel Island shells.

Packing checklist

Pack for the bay

Tick items off as you pack — or download the list to print and use on paper.

  • Sunscreen (reef-safe for beach days)
  • Hat and sunglasses
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Light rain jacket or poncho
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Swimsuit and beach towel
  • Light layers for air-conditioned museums
  • Phone charger and power bank
  • Small daypack for the Riverwalk
  • Insect repellent for park trails
  • Tickets and passes saved on your phone
  • A little room in the bag for souvenirs
