Eat & Shop

Food & drink

Eat like a Tampanian

Tampa's table was set by Cuban, Spanish and Italian kitchens in Ybor City — start with the icons, then graze the halls and markets.

Icon

The Cuban sandwich

Ham, roast pork, Genoa salami, Swiss, pickles and mustard on Cuban bread, pressed until crisp. Tampa's version — salami included — is the original.

Icon

Deviled crab & Spanish bean soup

Deviled crab — a seasoned crab croquette born in Ybor — and the Columbia's garbanzo-bean soup are the city's classic one-two lunch.

Icon

Grouper sandwich & stone crab

Grilled or blackened grouper sandwiches rule the beach cafés; stone crab claws are in season from mid-October to spring.

Halls & trucks

Food halls, trucks & markets

Graze Armature Works and Sparkman Wharf, chase the food-truck rallies, and shop the Ybor City Saturday Market for produce, art and snacks.

Coffee & sweet

Coffee, dessert & ice cream

Café con leche in Ybor, local roasters in Seminole Heights, and ice-cream stops along the Riverwalk and Hyde Park Village.

Good to know

Options, prices & reservations

Vegetarian and vegan menus are common in Seminole Heights and St. Pete; halal kitchens cluster near USF and Temple Terrace. Expect casual lunches around $10–18 and sit-down dinners around $20–40 per person. Book the Columbia and popular Hyde Park spots ahead on weekends.

Shopping

Bring a bigger bag

From luxury malls to hand-rolled cigars, Tampa shopping doubles as sightseeing.

Mall

International Plaza & Westshore Plaza

The bay area's two big malls sit minutes apart near the airport: luxury brands and Bay Street dining at International Plaza, everyday stores at Westshore.

District

Hyde Park Village & Ybor shops

Open-air boutiques and cafés in Hyde Park Village; cigar shops, vintage stores and local art along Ybor's 7th Avenue.

Souvenirs

Take Tampa home

Hand-rolled Ybor cigars, guava pastries, local hot sauce, Gasparilla beads, Cuban coffee and sponge souvenirs from Tarpon Springs day trips.

Outlets

Outlet shopping nearby

Tampa Premium Outlets in Lutz, about 25 minutes north, plus Ellenton Premium Outlets on the way to Sarasota.

Taste of Tampa

The sandwich with a city story.

Tampa’s Cuban sandwich brings together ham, roasted pork, Swiss cheese, pickles, mustard and Genoa salami—a local twist tied to the immigrant communities that built Ybor City.

Order it pressed. Pair it with café con leche.
